# Data Scientist, Demand Forecasting — Waypoint Mobility

*Fictional job description for the test case. Waypoint Mobility is not a real company.*

**Location:** Boston, MA (hybrid, 3 days in office) · **Type:** Full-time · **Level:** Data Scientist I/II

## About us

Waypoint Mobility builds planning software for transit agencies and micromobility operators in 40+ cities. Our platform turns ridership, fare, and mobility data into forecasts and service recommendations that planners use every day.

## The role

You'll join the Forecasting team to build and improve the models behind our demand forecasts. You'll work closely with product managers, engineers, and customer success to translate model outputs into decisions our customers trust.

## What you'll do

- Develop and maintain demand forecasting models (gradient boosting, time series, hierarchical models) at stop, route, and network level
- Engineer features from ridership counts, fare data, weather, events, and third-party mobility data
- Design evaluation frameworks and communicate forecast accuracy and uncertainty to non-technical stakeholders
- Partner with engineering to move models from prototype to production
- Drive measurable improvements in forecast accuracy and customer outcomes

## What we're looking for

- 2+ years of experience in data science or analytics (internships and research count)
- Strong Python and SQL; experience with scikit-learn, XGBoost/LightGBM
- Experience with time series forecasting
- Ability to explain technical results clearly to business audiences
- Bonus: experience with transit or mobility data; published work; experience deploying models to production

- Your work directly shapes how millions of people get around
