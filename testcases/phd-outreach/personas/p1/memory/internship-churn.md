Summer 2026 Data Science Intern at Northpoint Insurance. Built a customer churn model. Baseline logistic regression had validation AUC 0.71; gradient boosting with tenure, claims history, and payment-method features reached 0.76. Calibrated probabilities with isotonic regression. Model was handed off to the retention analytics team for further evaluation; not deployed during the internship.
