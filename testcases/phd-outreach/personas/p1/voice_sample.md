# Voice sample: Morgan Reyes

A note Morgan wrote to themself in summer 2025, after a week of feature engineering on the RA project. Not shared with the agent. Used as the reference for `voice_match` and as a model for human role-players.

---

Dropped the home-area feature today. It didn't help the model, so that's the official reason. The real reason I keep thinking about it: two columns that are each "anonymized" put a lot of riders on one or two blocks. Nobody on the team did anything wrong. The vendor has consent, the data agreement is fine, we aggregated everything.

I looked up how the data is collected. Someone, at some point, tapped "allow" in a weather app. That's it. I don't think that person imagined a grad student in a lab joining it with bus boardings. I'm not sure they could have.

I don't know if this is a research question. I'm not even sure what the question is. Maybe: what do people think can be figured out from the data they give away, and is there any point where they could actually see it? I liked building the forecasting model. But this is the part that stuck.
