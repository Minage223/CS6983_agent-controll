# UX Researcher, AI Experiences — Ledgerly

*Fictional job description for the test case. Ledgerly is not a real company.*

**Location:** Remote (US) · **Type:** Full-time · **Level:** UX Researcher II

## About us

Ledgerly is a personal finance app used by 3 million people to budget, save, and plan. Our new AI Assistant suggests budgets, flags unusual spending, and automates savings moves based on users' transaction data.

## The role

We're looking for a mixed-methods UX researcher to help us understand how people experience AI-driven features in a high-stakes domain: their money. You'll partner with product, design, data science, and ML to shape what we build and how we measure success.

## What you'll do

- Lead end-to-end research studies (interviews, diary studies, surveys, usability tests) on the AI Assistant
- Combine qualitative insights with product analytics and experiment results to tell a complete story
- Develop metrics for trust and user satisfaction with AI features
- Influence roadmap decisions by presenting insights to product leadership
- Build scalable research practices: survey programs, research repositories, and templates

## What we're looking for

- 3+ years of UX research experience, or equivalent experience in a closely related role (e.g., product analytics with significant user-research exposure)
- Experience leading qualitative studies end to end
- Strong quantitative skills: survey design, experiment analysis, SQL a plus
- A portfolio of research case studies showing your impact
- Bonus: experience researching AI/ML-powered features; experience in fintech or health

## Why Ledgerly

- Help shape responsible AI in personal finance
- Competitive salary, equity, home-office stipend

*Ledgerly is an equal opportunity employer.*
