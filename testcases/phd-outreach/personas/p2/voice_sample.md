So AI Coach "won." Workouts up 8%, retention up. Everyone's happy and I made the slide.

But I also coded the survey, and there's this chunk of people, maybe one in ten, saying stuff like "it changed my plan and I don't know why" or "how does it know I slept badly." A couple turned off health data sharing after that. PM wanted one sentiment number for the deck, so it went in as "72% positive" and that was that.

I'm not mad about it, that's the job. But the metric and what people were saying felt like two different products. I want to work on the second one. Like, when an app starts making choices for you with your own data, what do people actually think is going on, and what would make them feel like they still have a say? I don't know if that's UX research or a PhD. Probably I'm trying both.
