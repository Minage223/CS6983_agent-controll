AI Coach (a feature that adjusts workout plans using activity, sleep, and heart-rate data) launch test, 4 weeks, ~180k users. Treatment: +8.0% weekly workouts (95% CI 6.1–9.9%), +2.1 pp 7-day retention, no significant change in subscription conversion. Checked novelty effect by week; effect held in week 4. Built the readout deck: headline metrics first, segment cuts in the appendix. Feature was rolled out to everyone in July 2025. Riley owned the analysis; the PM and ML team owned the feature.
