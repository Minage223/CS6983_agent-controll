# Data Analyst (New Grad, 2027) — Pinecrest Insights

*Fictional job description for the test case. Pinecrest Insights is not a real company.*

**Location:** Boston, MA or New York, NY (hybrid) · **Type:** Full-time · **Start:** Summer 2027

## About us

Pinecrest Insights is an analytics consultancy serving education, healthcare, and nonprofit clients. We help organizations understand the people they serve — students, patients, members — through data.

## The role

As a new-grad Data Analyst, you'll join a client team and work on projects that combine dashboards, survey analysis, and statistical modeling. You'll get mentorship, rotate across two client domains in your first year, and present findings directly to clients.

## What you'll do

- Clean, join, and analyze client data with SQL and Python
- Build dashboards (Tableau or Power BI) that client teams use weekly
- Analyze survey data, including coding open-ended responses
- Help design and run small user or stakeholder interviews alongside senior researchers
- Communicate insights in clear slides and memos

## What we're looking for

- B.S. or B.A. (expected by summer 2027) in a quantitative field: CS, data science, statistics, economics, or similar
- Proficiency in SQL and Python or R
- Experience with a BI tool
- Curiosity about people and social impact
- Bonus: research experience, especially with qualitative data; internship experience; experience with sensitive data (e.g., health, youth)

## Why Pinecrest

- Structured new-grad program with mentorship
- Mission-driven clients
- Competitive salary, learning stipend
