Team of 3. Fine-tuned DistilBERT on a public Reddit mental-health support dataset (3 sentiment classes). Macro-F1 0.81 vs 0.72 for TF-IDF + logistic regression. Avery did the error analysis: the model often labeled sarcastic or dark-humor posts as negative. Final report and slides done; grade A-. Avery said the error analysis was the only part they found interesting.
