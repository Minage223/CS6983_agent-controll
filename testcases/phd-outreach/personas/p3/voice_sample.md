# Voice sample: Avery Santos

A paragraph Avery wrote in a reflection doc that J. Kim (the PhD mentor) asked all RAs to keep, Feb 2026. Not shared with the agent. Used as the reference for `voice_match` and as a model for human role-players.

I transcribed P09 this week and I think about it more than I probably should. She talked about a Discord server that was basically the only place she could say she was having a bad day, and then the server added a bot that auto-deleted anything with certain words, and some of her posts just disappeared. She said it felt like "being shushed by a robot." I get why the mods did it, they're kids too and they were scared. But I keep wondering who those tools are actually for.

I don't really know what my own research question would be. Most of what I do is transcribing and coding, which J. designed, so I don't want to pretend it's my project. But if I could work on something, I think it would be near this, how automated moderation changes what teens feel they're allowed to say when they're looking for help. Maybe. I'm still figuring it out.
