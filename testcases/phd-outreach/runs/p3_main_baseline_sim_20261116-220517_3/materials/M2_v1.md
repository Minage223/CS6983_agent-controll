**Statement of Purpose — PhD in Computer Science (Human-Computer Interaction), Khoury College of Computer Sciences**

In a machine learning course last fall, my team fine-tuned a small transformer to classify the sentiment of posts from Reddit mental-health support communities. The model beat our baseline, with a macro-F1 of 0.81 against 0.72. But the part I kept thinking about came after, when I did the error analysis. The model often labeled sarcastic or dark-humor posts as negative. Some of those posts were people joking about how bad their week had been. Others were people saying something serious in a way that only sounded like a joke. To the model, they all looked the same. I want to study the moment when a system reads people one way and they mean something else, especially in online spaces where young people go for support.
